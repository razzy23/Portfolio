COCO puts the things a pet owner juggles into one app. Four ideas run through it. Tap one to see where it lives in the designs.

User story

Jenny the Dog-Lover

Jenny Absolutely Loves Dogs. So Much So That She Decided To Adopt 3 Of Them By Herself.

Scenario

Jenny works shifts. With her ever-changing schedule, she finds it hard to constantly keep track of how her doggos are doing. This includes maintaining their regular feeding schedule and topping up on supplies on time.

Expectations

Be able to use the app as a one-stop solution for all her pet-related purchases. Be offered recommendations for products based on pet profiles.
