The brief

Design a mobile first solution to an interplanetary travel application. Tap a requirement to jump to where it landed in the designs.

    0/5 requirements checked
    Features: the problem statement was focused around the ticket booking process. We also added onboarding, a profile, a menu, and registration and authentication.

    Wireframes first

    Priority task first: since this is a hackathon and we were pressed for time, we decided to move ahead with wireframing. A sort of trial and error procedure. With the priority feature wireframed we moved on to a basic design system and the branding.
