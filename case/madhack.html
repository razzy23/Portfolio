MadHack 23

Brand overview

MadHack is a recurring mobile application development hackathon organised by the IEEE Student Branch of UCSC. It is an evolving brand built around innovation, creativity, technology, and collaboration.

Each edition may introduce a unique theme and visual direction, but the core MadHack identity should remain consistent and recognisable.

Every edition should build upon the legacy of previous events while contributing to the continued growth and recognition of the MadHack brand.
